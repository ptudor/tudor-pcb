Tudor PCB 1.0 is a native SwiftUI and Metal viewer for PCB fabrication
packages on macOS. It opens Gerber ZIP archives or extracted layer folders,
presents the board as an interactive 3D object, and calls attention to
fabrication details that deserve a human review.

## Downloads

- `TudorPCB-1.0.0-macos-universal.dmg`: installer with an Applications shortcut.
- `TudorPCB-1.0.0-macos-universal.zip`: the app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs, macOS 14 or later.
- `TudorPCB-1.0.0-macos-universal.dSYM.zip`: debug symbols matching the shipped executable.
- `TudorPCB-1.0.0-macos-universal.notarization.json`: accepted Apple submission IDs and final package hashes.
- `checksums.txt`: SHA-256 manifest for the release assets.

Open the installer or expand the app ZIP, then move Tudor PCB to Applications.
The app and installer are Developer ID signed, notarized by Apple, and carry
stapled notarization tickets. The app runs in the App Sandbox and reads only
the files and folders you select.

## What it understands

- RS-274X Gerber lines, flashes, circular interpolation, regions, dark and
  clear polarity, aperture macros, and panel step-repeat.
- Excellon plated and non-plated drill files, including explicit decimals,
  declared precision, and Altium and KiCad format comments.
- EasyEDA Pro, Eagle, KiCad/X2, and common Altium layer naming, with structured
  Gerber `FileFunction` attributes taking precedence.
- Direct ZIP opening with size limits and CRC verification, JLCPCB production
  review envelopes, and safely nested fabrication ZIPs.
- Multi-contour boards, routed cutouts, through holes and slots with physical
  bore walls, and EasyEDA breakaway panel rails.
- Color silkscreen proof images mapped to board coordinates, with EasyEDA's
  encrypted factory payloads detected and labeled rather than guessed.
- Persistent fabrication history with source age, inspection times, board
  statistics, generator details, search, and secure quick reopen.

## Limits

EasyEDA `.FCTS` and `.FCBS` color silkscreen payloads are encrypted for JLCPCB
and are not decrypted; the ordinary silkscreen is rendered as a labeled
fallback. Blind and buried machining, arc routes, and unsupported aperture macro
primitives reject the affected layer with a diagnostic instead of guessing
geometry. Integer Excellon coordinates without format information are rejected
with an explicit message. The app does not edit or copy fabrication sources.

The source is available under the MIT license, and the license text is
bundled inside the app. Published artifacts include checksums and GitHub
build attestations.
Attestations identify the build source; Apple signatures and notarization are
verified separately. See the repository's `docs/releases.md` for verification.
